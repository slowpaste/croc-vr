## Get started

1. Download the app for your computer below.
2. Open it and click **Choose .bin file…** to select your Croc PlayStation backup.
3. Click **Build for Steam Frame** or **Build for Facebook Shitface 3**.
4. When it finishes, click **Open finished apps** for your app and installation steps.

| Computer | File to download | How to open it |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | `croc-of-slop-vr-windows-x64.exe` | Double-click it. |
| Mac with Apple Silicon | `croc-of-slop-vr-macos-arm64.dmg` | Open it, drag the app into Applications, then open the app. |
| Mac with Intel | `croc-of-slop-vr-macos-x64.dmg` | Open it, drag the app into Applications, then open the app. |
| Linux (x64, Ubuntu 22.04 or newer) | `croc-of-slop-vr-linux-x64.tar.gz` | Extract it and open `croc-of-slop-vr`. |

Setup runs automatically and downloads a few GB the first time. Android setup asks you to accept Google's terms. You do not need Python or the source ZIP.

Your disc stays on your computer. Each headset app file is under 1 GB; allow more than 2 GB on the headset for installation.

Your computer may ask for first-launch approval because these downloads are unsigned.
